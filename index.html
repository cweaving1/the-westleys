Good to Know

What should I wear?

Sunday is black tie. Saturday's welcome party is Cape Cod cocktail. The ceremony is on the lawn and evenings by the water get cool, so bring a layer and skip the stilettos.

Can I bring a date?

We can only host the guests named on your invitation. If you have a plus-one, you'll see it when you RSVP.

Are kids invited?

We love your little ones, but this will be an adults-only weekend.

When do I need to RSVP?

By February 1, 2027. If something changes after that, please reach out to us directly.

Where should I stay?

See Travel & Stay. Both room blocks hold until June 1, 2027.

Is there parking?

Yes, Chatham Bars Inn has valet parking.
